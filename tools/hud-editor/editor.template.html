  1. Choose a screen at the top (a phone, a tablet or a PC) and a scene, like Mowing a job.
  2. Drag pieces into place. Pink lines show when edges line up. Pieces in a column swap places; drag a column by its name tab.
  3. Red stripes mean two pieces overlap, or a piece sits under Roblox's buttons.
  4. Press Save layout, then tell Claude to apply your HUD layout.
